Entry for the make.fun **Sum up 24 November's world news in one image** competition — one editorial image that sums up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026. The program copies the committed image to `/output/entry.png`, byte-for-byte, with a size guard, a whole-image format check, and a sha256 self-check.

## The day's news

The image covers the five stories that defined 24 November 2026:

- **Russian warplanes enter South Korea's air defence zone** — Seoul issues an emergency alert for Ulleung Island after unannounced incursions.
- **NATO sends AWACS early-warning aircraft to Poland** after three Russian missiles landed in the country, as the alliance calls an urgent meeting.
- **COP31 climate talks collapse in Turkey** — the summit ends without a consensus deal as the fossil-fuel phase-out stalls.
- **Ukraine sinks a Russian warship in Libya** — a corvette is destroyed by maritime drones in the port of Benghazi, days after a drone strike on a Russian cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland.
- **A candy factory fire in Indonesia kills 24 people**, with rescuers searching for missing workers.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to choose where `entry.png` is written (defaults to `/output`).

## The entry

`entry.png` — a 1024×1024 editorial collage: fighter jets over a misty Korean island coast, a NATO AWACS over Poland, the deserted COP31 hall in Istanbul, the burning corvette in Benghazi harbour, and firefighters at the Indonesian factory fire, under the masthead "TUESDAY 24 NOVEMBER 2026".
